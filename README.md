# Aditya & Richa | Digital Wedding Invitation 🪷

A cinematic, interactive digital wedding invitation celebrating the sacred union of **Aditya & Richa** along the holy ghats of Varanasi and The Munger Club.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

* **Holy Varanasi Temple Entrance**: 3D camera zoom transition entering through carved sandstone temple gates into the sacred sanctum.
* **Scroll-Driven Multi-Stage Temple Journey**: Continuous 3D dolly zoom into the temple archway (`1.0x -> 2.55x`), 3D parallax on carved stone pillars and brass bells, with dynamic phase cross-fades for couple names and family lineages.
* **Protected Petals Canvas Corridor**: Procedural falling rose and marigold petals restricted to side margins to preserve typography readability.
* **Sacred Ghats & Shubh Muhurat Countdown**: Real-time countdown timer with spring pop-in boxes, live seconds tick animation, and photographic depth parallax.
* **The Journey ("यहीं कहीं प्यार है")**: Realistic chain-suspended painted wooden signboard with pendulum breeze physics and gold foil sheen parchment story.
* **The Celebrations (2x2 Collage Grid)**: Mehndi, Matikor, Haldi, and Sangeet ceremony cards with staggered 3D tilt entrances, ink stamp date badges, and Google Calendar integration.
* **Barat & Saat Phere**: Procession card with festival lantern sway and glowing sacred flame mandap.
* **Munger Club Heritage Venue**: Royal palace destination showcase with dual hanging lanterns and Google Maps directions.
* **RSVP & Shower of Blessings**: Interactive RSVP with WhatsApp integration and live local-storage community blessings wall.
* **Indian Wedding Audio Engine**: Ambient temple bell synthesizer and soundtrack loop.
